## Design Context

### Users
Confirmed by the owner: businesses of all sizes, independent professionals, startups and partner agencies, in Lille, France and Europe. The site is bilingual French/English. Visitors evaluate expertise and completed projects, then contact Thermidor about their needs. One administrator manages portfolio projects.

### Brand Personality
Technical, trustworthy, simple, efficient.

### Aesthetic Direction
Confirmed priority: maximum navigation performance and sobriety. Calm, refined and editorial: warm off-white, restrained olive accents, ample typography, fine dividers and actual project screenshots. References supplied by the owner: morez.co, wearedaima.framer.website, igniteagency.com, oroya.fr. Keep motion brief and functional; no intro loaders, scroll hijacking, autoplay video, flashy gradients or decorative heavy JavaScript.

### Design Principles
- **Calm clarity**: Reduce noise and visual intensity; prioritize readable typography and clear hierarchy.
- **Trust through restraint**: Use consistent spacing, soft contrasts, and predictable interactions to signal reliability.
- **Purposeful accents**: Reserve color and motion for important calls-to-action; keep most surfaces neutral and quiet.
- **Refined details**: Use subtle textures, gentle shadows, and focused micro-interactions instead of dramatic effects.
